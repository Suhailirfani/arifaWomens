How Monthly Vehicle Fees Work in Darul Ma'arif Academy

Vehicle fees are monthly recurring fees, not yearly installments.

1. Fixed Monthly Rate Per Stop Each bus stop has a fixed monthly fee (e.g. Feroke Chungam: ₹1,200). Boarding students are charged this exact monthly fee.
2. Due Updates Every {{ setting.due_day_of_month }}st of Month Dues roll out automatically on the {{ setting.due_day_of_month }}st (or user-chosen date) of every month for all active bus pass holders.
3. Partial Usage Customizer If a student uses the bus partially (e.g. 10 days, one-way, medical leave), Admin can reduce an exact amount (₹) or reduce by percentage (%) for that month.
