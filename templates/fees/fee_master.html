How Installments & Automatic Next Dues Work

No technical setup required. You can manage your academy's entire fee breakdown directly from here:

1. Number of Installments & Amounts When creating an annual fee (e.g. ₹30,000), choose 2, 3, 4, or 10 installments or custom. Specify exact amounts for each term (e.g. ₹12,000 + ₹10,000 + ₹8,000).
2. When Does the Next Due Update Automatically? Installments automatically roll over based on calendar dates! As soon as an installment's due date passes, the next installment automatically becomes the Active Due Target across the dashboard and defaulters report.
