How Does the Next Due Date Update Automatically?

The academy system continuously aligns with the live calendar date:

  • Current Target: The system looks at all installments ordered by due date and automatically marks the upcoming installment whose due date is nearest to or on today's date.
  • Automatic Rollover: As soon as an installment's due date passes, the next installment automatically becomes the Active Due Target. The Fee Dashboard and Defaulters lists instantly switch to tracking the new due without any manual action needed!
  • Manual Flexibility: You can also manually lock or inspect any past or upcoming installment anytime in Fee Master or the Defaulters Tracker.
